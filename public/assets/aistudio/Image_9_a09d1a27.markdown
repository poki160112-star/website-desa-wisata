## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, hospitable, and ecologically immersive mobile-first visual experience for rural ecotourism. It channels the lush serenity of highland agricultural hills and mist-laden valley forests, balancing organic authenticity with contemporary mobile utility.

### Aesthetic Persona & Direction
- **Tactile Eco-Modernism:** Combines crisp typographic hierarchy, organic rounded geometry, and earthen color tones to evoke fresh mountain air, rich soils, and heartfelt village hospitality.
- **Tone:** Inviting, reliable, grounded, and vibrant without feeling commercial or sterile.
- **Experience Philosophy:** Optimized for on-the-go travelers navigating outdoors under direct sunlight. Visual elements prioritize readability, instant orientation, one-thumb interactions, and high-clarity transactional touchpoints (e.g., booking excursions, checking opening hours, direct local guide outreach).

## Colors

The palette balances lush montane foliage with earth, wood, and organic daylight surfaces to reinforce an outdoor sanctuary identity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors main navigations, key structural headers, solid interactive primary states, and focal branding. Evokes dense pine and canopy trees.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` - Warm Terracotta):** Earth and clay accents used for highlights, seasonal tour badges, price tags, and secondary calls-to-action that require warmth.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32` - Highland Emerald):** Supporting natural tone for progress indicators, availability states, active toggles, and eco-initiatives.
- **Neutral Light Base (`#F8FAF5` - Sand / Ivory Neutral):** Crisp canvas surface that prevents eye strain outdoors. Sub-surfaces use `#F3F4EE` (Soft Highland Mist) for subtle container separation.
- **Neutral Dark / Text (`#1F2937` - Slate Charcoal):** High-contrast, soft-black reading layer for long-form narrative, itinerary stops, and metadata.
- **Vibrant Functional Accents:**
  - **Fresh Mint Accent (`#A3E635`):** High-energy punch for new updates, eco-tags, and subtle edge highlights.
  - **Warm Amber (`#D97706`):** Ratings, operational alerts, weather notices, and sunset trail markers.
  - **Direct Contact Signal (`#25D366` - WhatsApp Vibrant Green):** Dedicated floating action contact channel ensuring instantaneous local concierge access.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project a clean, welcoming, and legible presence on high-density mobile screens. Its geometric forms with humanist terminal cuts ensure numerals, local place names, and price tiers render cleanly across handheld viewports.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Rules
- **Headline Scannability:** Headlines use `-0.01em` to `-0.03em` tracking to keep compound Indonesian terms compact and visually distinct.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy maintains a generous line height (`1.5` to `1.6x`) to facilitate comfortable reading during transit.
- **Badges and Metadata:** Microcopy uses `label-sm` and `label-md` with semi-bold to bold weights, keeping ticket prices, live operational statuses, and trail difficulties instantly clear.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first layout strategy designed around one-hand ergonomics, thumb swipe zones, and high fluid responsiveness.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile Grid (up to 639px):** Single-column dynamic flow with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Key interactive touch targets conform to a minimum height of 48px.
- **Tablet Grid (640px to 1023px):** 6-column fluid structure with 1.25rem margins and gutters. Destination cards reflow into 2-column masonry or symmetrical pairs.
- **Desktop Grid (1024px+):** 12-column bounded layout constrained to a maximum width of 1200px, flanked by `margin-desktop: 2.5rem` to preserve deliberate, grounded framing.

### Spacing Principles
- Compact components (chips, badges, metadata rows) utilize `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Content containers, card paddings, and field groupings rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).
- Distinct thematic modules (e.g., packages, village artisans, cultural events) separate using `space-xl` (32px) on mobile and 48px to 64px on wide screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows in favor of ambient mountain light, soft atmospheric groundings, and tactile layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** Base background tone `#F8FAF5` providing an earthy, matte ground.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers / Cards):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or softened `#F3F4EE` with an ultra-faint tinted border (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`) and an ambient, warm shadow (`0px 4px 20px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05)`).
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Navbars & Modal Sheets):** Frosted glass effect with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background fill `rgba(248, 250, 245, 0.88)`, and a clean bottom border of `1px solid rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.1)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Action Controls / Floating WhatsApp Button):** Direct tactile lift using a tinted, buoyant glow: `0px 10px 25px -4px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)` to command immediate thumb access without visually overwhelming page content.

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous, friendly radii inspired by river pebbles, terraced hills, and hand-carved woodwork.

### Shape Scales & Application
- **Base Components (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Standard input fields, event accordions, alert notices, and itinerary segment nodes.
- **Major Cards & Visual Containers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Destination picture cards, homestay packages, and interactive map frames.
- **Mobile Bottom Sheets (`rounded-t-2xl` / 32px):** Booking drawers and filter dialogs sliding upward from the bottom viewport.
- **Pills / Full Radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status indicators, amenity tags, circular iconography, and the floating WhatsApp outreach button.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#1B4D3E`) with white text, minimum height of 48px, horizontal padding of 24px, and `rounded-xl`. Active state shifts subtly to `#14382D` with a scale down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a solid 1.5px border in `#1B4D3E` or warm Terracotta (`#C86D51`), paired with matching bold typography.
- **WhatsApp Quick-Connect Action:** Floating circular (56x56px) or bottom-docked pill button with solid background `#25D366`, pure white SVG icon, high-contrast label, and gentle pulsing shadow to invite instant questions.

### 2. Destination & Homestay Cards
- **Card Envelope:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, subtle border (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`), overflow hidden.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 16:10 with smooth zoom on interaction. Includes an absolute top badge row (Status & Category) and a bottom gradient vignette.
- **Content Block:** Destination title (`headline-sm`), distance/location tag with icon, review chip with amber star, and footer showcasing price per person beside a compact primary CTA.

### 3. Status Badges & Chips
- **Operational Badges (Jam Buka):** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent background with bold text. 
  - *Open:* Background `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.12)`, text `#2E7D32`.
  - *Closing Soon / Limited:* Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, text `#D97706`.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape, minimum height 38px for effortless thumb tapping. Inactive state: `#F3F4EE` with neutral text. Active state: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4D3E`) with white text and `Fresh Mint` dot icon.

### 4. Form Fields & Interactive Inputs
- **Inputs:** Height 50px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E5E7EB`, padding horizontal 16px, `rounded-lg`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#1B4D3E` with an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.15)`.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios & Date Pickers
- **Pickers:** Large target zones (min 44px) with custom radio dials colored in `#1B4D3E` and `#A3E635` inner checks.
- **Calendar & Availability Indicators:** Highlight selected dates with deep forest background circles, while unavailable slots display soft strikethroughs on `#F3F4EE`.

### 6. Village Experience Itinerary Lists
- Vertical connecting line using `#C86D51` at 20% opacity. Step indicators feature solid mini-circles with warm terracotta centers, accompanying title, timestamp, and local tips card.
